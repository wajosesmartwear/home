Our Story & Identity

About Wajose Smart Wear

Mission & Vision

Empowering Elegance, Creating Opportunities

Wajose Smart Wear Ltd was founded with a singular purpose: to make premium smart wear accessible to every Kenyan professional while creating meaningful employment opportunities across the country.

From humble beginnings, we have expanded into major urban centers including Nairobi, Nakuru, Eldoret, and now Kericho. Our stores offer carefully curated fashion lines ranging from formal suits to everyday smart casual outfits.

Unmatched Quality

Every garment passes rigorous quality checks before reaching our showroom shelves.

Customer First

Our floor helpers and cashiers are trained to deliver personalized styling assistance.

Youth Empowerment

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my job application?

Go to the Careers page, scroll down to the Application Tracker, enter your National ID number, and click Search Record.

What is the application fee for?

The processing fee (KES 80) covers automated background verification and prevents spam applications. Paid via M-Pesa Till Number 1601371.

Can I apply for multiple positions?

Yes, you may submit individual applications per role using your National ID number.

When is the Kericho branch launch?

Staff orientation for Kericho begins immediately after shortlisting. Final store opening is scheduled for later this month.
